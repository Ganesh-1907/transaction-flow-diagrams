Built URL

Payment step link rx builds it after step 2. Any link with ?ref= opens step 3:
<rx origin>/<locale>/programs/<program_id>?ref=<registration_id>

registration_id is the participant's registration UUID, returned by the step 2 registration call. rx also sends it to CAPS as participant_id when Pay now is clicked.

Thank you link After the gateway, success or processing goes to:
<rx origin>/<locale>/programs/<program_id>/thankyou?ref=<registration_id>&caps_transaction_id=<CAPS id>

Fail link Failed, or any other status, goes to:
<rx origin>/<locale>/programs/<program_id>/failed?ref=<registration_id>&caps_transaction_id=<CAPS id>

Retry link Sent in the retry email (built by BX):
<participant retry_payment_url>/retry-payment?ref=<registration_id>&caps_transaction_id=<CAPS id>

retry_payment_url is saved on the participant at step 2 (rx sends <rx origin>/<locale>/programs/<program_id>). The Try again button on the failed page opens the same retry-payment page.

The links never change. If the payment is already finished, opening the step 3 link goes to the thank-you page.

Step 3, click 1program_participant_transactionINSERT T1 PENDING, then FAILED (rescue service)
Step 3, click 2program_participant_transactionINSERT T2 PENDING, then FAILED (declined)
Step 3, click 3program_participant_transactionINSERT T3 PENDING, then CONFIRMED
Step 3, click 3program_participantUPDATE P1, balance_due reduced, payment_status CONFIRMED
  • Step 1 writes the users row.
  • Step 2 inserts the program_participant row.
  • Every Pay now click in step 3 creates one more transaction row.
  • T1 abandoned: PENDING, later FAILED by the rescue service.
  • T2 declined: FAILED.
  • T3 paid: CONFIRMED.
  • After a failed attempt, the retry email link opens step 3 again.
