Built URL

Payment step link Step 3 link (rx builds it after step 2). Any link with ?ref= opens step 3:
<rx origin>/<locale>/programs/<program_id>?ref=<registration_id>

The same link pays the balance later. Step 3 reads the registration: while the deposit is not paid it charges deposit_amount with purpose DEPOSIT, after the deposit it charges balance_due with purpose REMAINING_AMOUNT.

Payment step link Complete Payment button on the thank-you page opens the same step 3 link. The deposit email link, built by BX, is:
<retry_payment_url>?ref=<registration_id>

retry_payment_url is saved on the participant at step 2 (rx sends <rx origin>/<locale>/programs/<program_id>).

Thank you link After the gateway, success or processing goes to:
<rx origin>/<locale>/programs/<program_id>/thankyou?ref=<registration_id>&caps_transaction_id=<CAPS id>

Fail link Failed, or any other status, goes to:
<rx origin>/<locale>/programs/<program_id>/failed?ref=<registration_id>&caps_transaction_id=<CAPS id>

Retry link Try again on the failed page opens the retry page. The deposit failed email, built by BX, links to the same page:
<retry_payment_url>/retry-payment?ref=<ref>&caps_transaction_id=<CAPS id>

In the email link, BX picks ref as the participant code when the participant has one, otherwise the registration id.

The links never change. If the payment is already finished (PROCESSING, or CONFIRMED with balance_due 0), opening the step 3 link goes to the thank-you page.

StepTableRows
Step 1usersINSERT user (or UPDATE if the email already exists)
Step 2, Pay Depositprogram_participantINSERT P1: deposit_type = DEPOSIT, deposit_amount = the deposit, total_amount = full amount, balance_due = full amount
Step 3, deposit paidprogram_participant_transaction, program_participantINSERT T (PENDING), then CONFIRMED; UPDATE P1: balance_due = balance_due - deposit paid
Step 3, deposit declinedprogram_participant_transactionINSERT T (PENDING), then FAILED. Pay now again inserts a new T
Balance paidprogram_participant_transaction, program_participantINSERT T (PENDING), then CONFIRMED; UPDATE P1: balance_due = balance_due - amount paid (becomes 0)
Balance declinedprogram_participant_transactionINSERT T (PENDING), then FAILED. Try again inserts a new T
  • Deposit registration: the participant pays a part now (deposit_amount) and the rest later (balance_due).
  • At step 2, balance_due is the full amount, the same as total_amount. It drops only when the deposit is paid.
  • Every Pay now click creates a new transaction row. A failed row is never reused.
  • A failed deposit sends the deposit failed email whatever the balance. A failed balance payment sends no email: the failure email needs balance_due to equal total_amount, and after the deposit it does not.
  • The balance is paid online with the thank-you button, the email link or the Complete Payment popup. The teacher recording the balance as an offline payment is not drawn here.
