Group Event Registration - Free, Paid and Paid with zero fee

Group of 3 (primary + 2 members): every person gets a users row and a program_participant row, but only the primary can get a program_participant_transaction row. Free: none. Paid: one, created by the payment webhook with the whole group's amount. Special case, paid with a zero total: one CONFIRMED row written by BX.

Built URL

Payment step link PAID: rx builds it after step 2. Any link with ?ref= opens step 3:
<rx origin>/<locale>/programs/<program_id>?ref=<registration_id>

registration_id is the primary's registration UUID, returned by the register call. rx sends it to CAPS as participant_id, with the group total as the amount.

Thank you link PAID: after the gateway, success, processing or pay later goes to:
<rx origin>/<locale>/programs/<program_id>/thankyou?ref=<registration_id>&caps_transaction_id=<CAPS id>

Thank you link FREE and ZERO FEE: Submit goes straight to the thank-you page. There is no payment, so there is no CAPS id:
<rx origin>/<locale>/programs/<program_id>/thankyou?ref=<registration_id>

Fail link PAID: failed, or any other status, goes to:
<rx origin>/<locale>/programs/<program_id>/failed?ref=<registration_id>&caps_transaction_id=<CAPS id>

Retry link PAID: the Try again button on the failed page opens:
<rx origin>/<locale>/programs/<program_id>/retry-payment?ref=<registration_id>&caps_transaction_id=<CAPS id>

Member action link Each member's email has this link. It opens the page where the member confirms the agreement (no payment):
<program registration_url>/action-required?ref=<member registration_id>

The links never change. If the payment is already finished, opening the step 3 link goes to the thank-you page.

  • Group of 3 in this example: the primary (P1) and 2 members (P2, P3). Each person gets a users row and a program_participant row. The members point at the primary (primary_participant_id).
  • Only the primary can ever get a program_participant_transaction row. The payment is made for the whole group under the primary's registration id.
  • FREE: one step, one call for all 3 people. No payment, no CAPS order, no webhook, so no transaction row for anyone.
  • PAID: the 3 rows are saved before payment and stay hidden until the first webhook. Each member's total_amount is their own fee. The primary's balance_due is the whole group's balance.
  • PAID: Pay now sends the group total. Every click creates one more transaction row, on the primary only, with the whole group's amount. T1 abandoned is PENDING, later FAILED by the rescue service. T2 declined is FAILED. T3 paid is CONFIRMED.
  • PAID: when T is CONFIRMED the primary's balance_due is reduced (0 when the group paid in full). The members' own rows are not changed by the payment.
  • SPECIAL CASE, zero fee: the group total is 0 (fee level price 0, or a discount code brings it to 0). Steps 1 and 2 are the same as a paid group, but the Step 2 button reads Submit and there is no step 3.
  • SPECIAL CASE: BX writes ONE CONFIRMED transaction, on the primary only, in the same request that inserts the 3 participants. Only the primary is set to CONFIRMED. The members stay PENDING.
  • Members: each gets a pending-action email with the Member action link. They confirm their agreement on the action-required page. That updates only their own row. They are never asked to pay.

Group registration (rx pages for a primary participant who adds members). Single-participant events are in the other Event Registration flow. Ticketed events have their own pages.

Code-derived (rx, bx-be-nestjs and caps-be on origin/dev), not run on a live system.

Not in the repositories, so assumed: the database trigger that sets payment_status CONFIRMED on the primary after a webhook. Nothing in the code updates the members' payment_status after payment, so it stays PENDING unless a trigger does it. The primary's email in a free group is the pending-action one only if no trigger confirms it first.

Not checked: whether members also get tickets or invoices, whether a member is ever sent to a payment step, and whether the primary is told when the last member finishes.

Possible gaps, from reading the code: the zero-fee Submit button looks at the primary's own amount while the redirect looks at the group sum. A registration saved again with an existing registration id does not create the zero-fee transaction.
